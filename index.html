About Me

Hello, I am Donghoon Kim, a graduate student in the integrated MS/Ph.D. program in the Department of Mathematical Sciences at KAIST. I am also a student researcher in the Discrete Mathematics Group (DIMAG) at the Institute for Basic Science (IBS). My advisor is Andreas Holmsen.

I am interested in objects that have both combinatorial and geometric interpretations. Currently, I am studying (1) intersection patterns of convex hulls and (2) polytopality of simplicial complexes.
